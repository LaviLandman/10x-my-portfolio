About Me

I serve as the Director of Program Management at Ibex Medical Analytics, pioneer of AI-powered clinical diagnostics in digital pathology. My focus sits at the intersection of medical device regulatory compliance, engineering velocity, and AI innovation.

I lead cross-functional program planning and release execution across 14 specialized teams (including Algorithm, Backend, Clinical, Data, DevOps, QARA, SQA, and Web). Beyond traditional program management, I architect and build production automations, AI agent ecosystems, and audit-grade regulatory frameworks that multiply engineering throughput and guarantee strict compliance.
